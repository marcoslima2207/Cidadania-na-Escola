# Imagens do redesign

As imagens decorativas foram criadas com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen. Não representam integrantes reais do projeto. Os originais foram preservados e as versões WebP foram otimizadas para o site sem instalar dependências no frontend.

- `frontend/public/illustrations/student-hero.webp`: estudante no hero, 900 × 1080, cerca de 159 KB.
- `frontend/public/illustrations/learning-together.webp`: ilustração educativa, 900 × 600, cerca de 89 KB.
- `frontend/public/avatars/portrait-01.webp` a `portrait-08.webp`: cópias otimizadas dos oito JPGs existentes, 256 px, cerca de 44 KB no total. Os originais e os identificadores persistidos foram preservados; o navegador escolhe WebP por `srcset`.

## Prompt do hero

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent cutout for the right column of a Brazilian educational website hero. Primary request: editorial photograph of a smiling young adult Brazilian Black woman student, curly shoulder-length dark hair, simple white T-shirt, dark navy backpack straps, holding two closed study notebooks in institutional yellow #E7B400 and royal blue #002D9A. Waist-up portrait, body slightly angled toward the left and looking thoughtfully up-left toward the heading. Friendly, natural, realistic skin texture, soft daylight. Isolated subject on a genuinely transparent background, clean edges around hair, no backdrop and no shadow outside the subject. Subject fills the image, leave small margin above head, complete arms and notebooks visible. No text, no logos, no watermark. Intended to sit over a blue hero with geometric decorations made separately in CSS.

## Prompt da ilustração

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent educational illustration for the right side of quiz and about banners on a Brazilian civic learning platform. Primary request: polished friendly flat editorial illustration of two diverse adult students learning together, a young woman with wavy dark hair wearing royal blue #002D9A and an adult man with medium brown skin wearing mustard yellow #E7B400, both holding books and discussing a new idea. A simple small lightbulb near their heads. Waist-up balanced compact composition, both people fully legible at small banner size, warm inclusive faces, clean outlines and subtly shaded forms, white accents and green #0A901C details. Genuinely transparent background, no scene or rectangle behind figures. No text, no logos, no watermark. These are generic illustrative students, not portraits of any real project team.
